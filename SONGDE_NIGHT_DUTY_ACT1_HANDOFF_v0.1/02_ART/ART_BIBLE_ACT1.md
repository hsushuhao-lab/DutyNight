# ACT 1 ART BIBLE — Warm Normality

## Core statement
Act 1 must look like a place where a tired physician could still feel safe.
The horror works only if this warmth is later lost.

## Style
- Photorealistic, cinematic realism.
- Contemporary Taiwanese psychiatric hospital atmosphere, but fictionalized.
- Clean but lived-in; not luxury, not abandoned.
- Soft practical light, glass nursing-station partitions, wood/neutral surfaces, real clutter.
- Avoid excessive bloom, orange-teal blockbuster grading, or overt horror props.

## Color / light
- Sunset warm gold from windows.
- Interior warm-white practicals around desks and duty room.
- Neutral fluorescent light in service corridors.
- After 20:30, sunset disappears but the scene should still feel safe.

## Duty room
Must be a **private independent room with a real door**.
Interior language:
- Single bed
- Bedside lamp
- Desk + computer/phone
- Small storage
- Coat hook / white coat
- Simple personal items
- Warm lamp creates sanctuary feeling

## Closed ward
- Secure doors and controlled access are visible but not sensationalized.
- Nursing station is open to workflow and observation.
- Patients are treated as people, not horror decoration.

## Character consistency
Lee in Act 1:
- white coat
- beige/light khaki shirt
- dark trousers
- glasses
- pens in coat pocket
- calm, approachable expression

The “other” presence must not be named or visually emphasized in Act 1. If it appears in a reference composition, it should only be a background ambiguity or reflection.
