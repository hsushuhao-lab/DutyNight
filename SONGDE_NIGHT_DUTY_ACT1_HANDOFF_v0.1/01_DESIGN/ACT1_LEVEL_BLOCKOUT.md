# ACT 1 LEVEL BLOCKOUT

## Playable locations
Only build the minimum topology needed for Act 1.

### 3F — Administration / physician work area
Required:
- Elevator lobby
- Generic physician/admin corridor
- Key office (fictionalized room number in game)
- Duty log interaction point
- 2–3 eligible computer workstations

### 4F — Closed psychiatric ward cluster
Required:
- Elevator lobby
- Locked/controlled ward entry feel
- Nursing station hub
- Independent duty room with a real door and private-room feeling
- Short representative ward corridors for 4A/4B/4C/4D topology
- Workstation area
- At least one treatment/procedure interaction point

### 2F — ER connector (lightweight in Act 1)
Required only if the priority branch sends the player to ER:
- Elevator arrival
- Short ER corridor
- Nursing station
- Generic consult point

## Compression rule
Do not reconstruct the real hospital 1:1.
Compress walking distances to preserve pacing.

Suggested first-play traversal targets:
- 3F admin → elevator: 20–35 s
- 4F elevator → nursing station: 10–20 s
- Nursing station → duty room: 10–20 s
- Nursing station → far ward branch: 25–40 s
- ER arrival → consult point: 20–35 s

## Warm lighting rules
- Warm practicals around nursing station and duty room.
- Sunset contribution strongest at 17:00 and fades by 19:30.
- Avoid dirty horror grading in Act 1.
- Closed ward can still be secure/clinical without looking threatening.
