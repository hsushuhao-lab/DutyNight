# Annie final identity — V2.0 canon

Annie is a late-1990s CPR training mannequin used by the fictional hospital and nicknamed 「安妮」. She is not a deceased female doctor, not 張守恆, and not the primary aggressor. Do not reproduce a real manufacturer or brand.

Annie is a nameless body and memory vessel produced by the 1998 loop. The hospital stripped 張守恆 of professional identity and converted him into a patient; his yellowed white coat and engraved stethoscope were later left on/with the training mannequin. The actual hostile force is the hospital's identity-overwrite mechanism and the false 李醫師 Doppelgänger.

## Physical design

- Clearly recognizable old CPR training mannequin, never a ghost-faced human figure.
- Smooth molded vinyl face, fine jaw/neck seams, fixed unfocused eyes, nearly blank expression, and a small blow-training airway opening.
- Short untidy wig-like hair, long rigid proportions, and visible shoulder, elbow, wrist, knee, and finger teaching hinges.
- Slightly oversized yellowed white coat with cloth-like roughness and a blank/removed badge area. Coat belongs to 張守恆.
- Aged stethoscope hangs at the neck. Its metal plate reads exactly 「祝 守恆 醫師 1997 執業誌慶」. It belongs to 張守恆, not Annie.
- CPR compression clicks sound like a stiff spring/training mechanism, never gore or bone-breaking.
