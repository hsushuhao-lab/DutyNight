# Annie final identity — V2.0 canon

Annie is a late-1990s CPR training mannequin used by the fictional hospital and nicknamed 「安妮」. She is not a deceased female doctor, not 張守恆, and not the primary aggressor. Do not reproduce a real manufacturer or brand.

Annie is a nameless body and memory vessel produced by the 1998 loop. The hospital stripped 張守恆 of professional identity and converted him into a patient; his yellowed white coat stayed on the training mannequin while his engraved stethoscope was staged separately nearby. The actual hostile force is the hospital's identity-overwrite mechanism and the false 李醫師 Doppelgänger.

## Physical design

- Clearly recognizable old CPR training mannequin, never a ghost-faced human figure.
- Smooth molded vinyl face with one small nose only, fine jaw/neck seams, and no eyes, sockets, brows, pupils, lashes, mouth, or airway opening.
- Short untidy wig-like hair, long rigid proportions, and visible shoulder, elbow, wrist, knee, and finger teaching hinges.
- Slightly oversized yellowed white coat with cloth-like roughness and a blank/removed badge area. Coat belongs to 張守恆.
- Annie's chest is clear of attached props, badges, pockets, buttons, and jewelry. 張守恆's separate stethoscope clue reads exactly 「祝 守恆 醫師 1997 執業誌慶」 and rests by the 3F training cart and at the M5 relic site.
- CPR compression clicks sound like a stiff spring/training mechanism, never gore or bone-breaking.
